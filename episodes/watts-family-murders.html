Watts Family Murders

November 17, 2024  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

The Watts family murders occurred in August 2018 in Frederick, Colorado. Chris Watts, the father, killed his pregnant wife, Shanann, and their two young daughters, Bella and Celeste. The crime gained significant media attention due to its horrific nature and the subsequent investigation. Watts initially denied involvement but later confessed to the murders. He was sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole. The case continues to be a source of fascination and discussion for true crime enthusiasts.
