A Hero at the Wilmington DMV: The Heroism of Corporal Matthew Snook

December 26, 2025

Welcome to Crime Time Snacks. I'm your host, Cory. Today, we're discussing a tragic and heroic event that unfolded just days ago in New Castle, Delaware.

On the afternoon of December 23rd, 2025, the Karen L. Johnson Division of Motor Vehicles was busy with customers preparing for the holiday season. Among them was Corporal Matthew Snook, a dedicated member of the Delaware State Police, working an overtime assignment at the reception desk. Corporal Snook was a respected officer who had served his community with distinction, and on this day, his actions would define his legacy forever.

At approximately 2:00 p.m., the routine was shattered. A 44-year-old man named Rahman Rose entered the building and, without warning, approached Corporal Snook from behind and opened fire. The sudden violence shocked everyone present. In an instant, a routine day at the DMV became a scene of tragedy.

What happened next is a testament to the courage of those who serve. Despite being mortally wounded, Corporal Snook's first instinct was to protect others. He pushed a DMV employee out of the line of fire and shouted for them to run, even as the suspect continued to fire. This act of selflessness, performed in his final moments, saved that employee's life. Corporal Snook exemplified the highest ideals of law enforcement: putting others before himself.

Rose allowed other customers to flee but remained inside, waiting for the inevitable arrival of law enforcement. When officers arrived, a gun battle ensued. A New Castle County police officer eventually ended the threat, firing through a window to stop the suspect. Rahman Rose died at the scene, ending the immediate danger.

Tragically, Corporal Snook was rushed to a local hospital, but his injuries were too severe. He passed away later that day, leaving behind a legacy of ultimate sacrifice and heroism. His death sent shockwaves through the Delaware State Police and the entire community.

A second trooper who responded to the scene sustained non-life-threatening injuries. Two civilians in the building were also injured, though not by gunshot wounds. The incident left many traumatized, but it also reminded us of the bravery of those who put on a uniform every day to protect us.

The Delaware State Police Homicide Unit continues to investigate the incident. Authorities are still working to understand the motive behind Rahman Rose's actions. What led him to commit this senseless act of violence? Was there a personal connection to the DMV or to Corporal Snook? These questions remain unanswered, but the investigation continues.

Corporal Matthew Snook's heroism on December 23rd, 2025, will not be forgotten. He died as he lived—serving others, protecting the innocent, and putting his community first. His family, his fellow officers, and the people of Delaware have lost a true hero.

In this episode, we look at the details of the investigation and the community's response to this senseless act of violence. This is the story of a hero at the Wilmington DMV.
