Ted Bundy and the Murder of Kimberly Leach

November 6, 2026  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

I'm Cory, and this week on CrimeTimeSnacks, we are looking at the devastating timeline of Ted Bundy's crimes and his eventual capture in Florida. From his early murders in Washington and Utah to his brazen escape from a Colorado jail, Bundy left a trail of terror across the country. He used a fake arm sling and a charming demeanor to lure young women to his Volkswagen Beetle, vanishing them into the night. But his arrival in Florida in 1978 brought a new level of horror when a twelve-year-old girl disappeared from her school. We'll walk through the timeline, the escapes, and the stolen car in Pensacola that finally brought his cross-country reign to a halt.
