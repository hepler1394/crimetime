Murders in Moscow

March 16, 2025  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

In this gripping episode of Crime Time Snacks , we delve into the harrowing case of the Idaho student murders that rocked the nation. On November 13, 2022, four University of Idaho students — Ethan Chapin, Madison Mogen, Xana Kernodle, and Kaylee Goncalves — were tragically murdered in their off-campus rental home in Moscow, Idaho. This episode unpacks the chilling events, the ongoing investigation, and the complexities surrounding the case.
