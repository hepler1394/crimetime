Amanda Knox and the Murder of Meredith Kercher

November 24, 2026  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

Meredith Kercher, a British exchange student, was stabbed to death in November 2007 in the Perugia, Italy apartment she shared with American student Amanda Knox. The episode follows Knox from Seattle to Via della Pergola 7, through the weeks before the killing and the morning Kercher's body was found. It then traces the court rulings that convicted Knox in 2009, overturned that conviction in 2011 and convicted her again in 2014, leading to the final decision in 2015.
