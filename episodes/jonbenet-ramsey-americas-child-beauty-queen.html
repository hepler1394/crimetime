JonBenét Ramsey Americas Child Beauty Queen

November 18, 2022  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

In Boulder Colorado Just a day after Christmas in 1996, JonBenét Ramsey a six-year child pageant star of the late 90's, was found dead in her basement. She was beaten and strangled. Hours after her parents, John and Patsy, reported her missing. Hours before, her mom had found a ransom note sitting on a staircase in the house. There have never been any arrests or named suspects in this case, even 26 years later. Tune in September 20th for a 2 part special where we go back to Christmas 1996 and relook at all the facts in this case.
