JonBenét Ramsey Americas Child Beauty Queen

November 18, 2022  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

In Boulder, Colorado, just a day after Christmas in 1996, JonBenét Ramsey, a six-year-old child pageant star, was found dead in her basement. She had been beaten and strangled. That was hours after her parents, John and Patsy, reported her missing, and hours after her mom found a ransom note on a staircase in the house. Nobody has ever been convicted in this case, even 26 years later. This is the lead-in to a two-part special where we go back to Christmas 1996 and look again at all the facts in this case.
