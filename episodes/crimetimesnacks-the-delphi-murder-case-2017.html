CrimeTimeSnacks: The Delphi Murder Case 2017

November 18, 2022  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

On February 14, 2017, the bodies of Abigail Williams (June 23, 2003 – February 13, 2017) and Liberty German (December 27, 2002 – February 13, 2017) were discovered near the Monon High Bridge Trail. The case has had no prominent suspects until recently. On October 28th police announced they had made an arrest in the case. A 50 year old Delphi Resident was arrested and charged with two felony counts of murder. Listen while we dive into the case starting over 5 years ago in on a beautiful hiking trail.
