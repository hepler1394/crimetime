JonBenét Ramsey | The Facts 96-22 |Part 1

November 20, 2022  ·  Cory

Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts

JonBenét Patricia Ramsey was murdered. That is the only certain thing about this case after 26 years. Their is key evidence in this case as well as more theories than their are people involved. I took weeks of research and broke it down into a 2 part segment where I go over all the facts known to the case today as well as some key suspects. I try to stay away from the theories that have little to know factual basis. Listen to the facts of the case and make your own opinion.
