Murders in Moscow

In this gripping episode of Crime Time Snacks , we delve into the harrowing case of the Idaho student murders that rocked the nation. Watts Family Murders

November 17, 2024

The Watts family murders occurred in August 2018 in Frederick, Colorado. Chris Watts, the father, killed his pregnant wife, Shanann, and their two young daughters, Bella and Celeste. The crime gained significant media attention due to its horrific nature and the subsequent investigation. Watts initially denied involvement but later confessed to the murders. He was sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole. The case continues to be a source of fascination and discussion for true crime enthusiasts.

JonBenét Ramsey Americas Child Beauty Queen

November 18, 2022

In Boulder Colorado Just a day after Christmas in 1996, JonBenét Ramsey a six-year child pageant star of the late 90's, was found dead in her basement. She was beaten and strangled. Hours after her parents, John and Patsy, reported her missing. Hours before, her mom had found a ransom note sitting on a staircase in the house. There have never been any arrests or named suspects in this case, even 26 years later. CrimeTimeSnacks: The Delphi Murder Case 2017

November 18, 2022

On February 14, 2017, the bodies of Abigail Williams (June 23, 2003 – February 13, 2017) and Liberty German (December 27, 2002 – February 13, 2017) were discovered near the Monon High Bridge Trail. The case has had no prominent suspects until recently. On October 28th police announced they had made an arrest in the case. A 50 year old Delphi Resident was arrested and charged with two felony counts of murder. Listen while we dive into the case starting over 5 years ago in on a beautiful hiking trail.
