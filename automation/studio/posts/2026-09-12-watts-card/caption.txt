August 13, 2018. Shanann Watts, 34 and fifteen weeks pregnant, and her daughters Bella, 4, and Celeste, 3, were reported missing from their house in Frederick, Colorado.

The next day her husband stood outside that house and told a local reporter he wanted them back. He had killed all three the previous morning.

Chris Watts pleaded guilty on November 6, 2018 and was sentenced on November 19 to three consecutive life terms without the possibility of parole, plus 84 years. He is held at Dodge Correctional Institution in Wisconsin. He will not be released.
