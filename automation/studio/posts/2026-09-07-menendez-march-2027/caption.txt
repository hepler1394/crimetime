Thirty-six years in, and the date just moved a year closer.

August 20, 1989. Jose and Kitty Menendez were shot in the den of their Beverly Hills home. Their sons Erik and Lyle were convicted in 1996 and sentenced to life without the possibility of parole.

In May 2025 a judge resentenced them to 50 years to life, which made them eligible for parole for the first time. In August 2025 the board denied both of them for three years.

This month the California parole board advanced their next hearing by more than a year. They are now set to go before it in March 2027. An earlier hearing is not a release. It is an earlier hearing.
