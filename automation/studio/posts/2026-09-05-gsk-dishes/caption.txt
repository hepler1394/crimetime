He stacked dishes on the husband's back.

Sacramento County, late 1970s. A man in a ski mask broke into homes at night, tied up the couple inside, and balanced plates on the man's back. If he heard them rattle, he said, everyone in the house would die.

He did it in home after home. Then the attacks turned into murders down the coast. Then, in 1986, he stopped. For 32 years nobody knew his name.

In 2018 a DNA match on a genealogy website gave it to them: Joseph James DeAngelo. Former police officer. 72 years old. Still living in Citrus Heights, in the same suburbs he had terrorized.
