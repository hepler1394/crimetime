The verdict is gone. The case is not.

March 2, 2023: a Colleton County jury convicted Alex Murdaugh of murdering his wife, Maggie, and their son, Paul. He was sentenced to two consecutive life terms.

May 13, 2026: the South Carolina Supreme Court unanimously threw out the murder convictions and ordered a new trial, citing jury interference by the county clerk of court, Becky Hill.

He is still in prison on his state and federal sentences for financial crimes, which he pleaded guilty to. On the murders he is presumed innocent again unless a new jury finds otherwise. He has denied killing Maggie and Paul from the start.

The retrial is set for April 5, 2027, moved to Sumter County.
