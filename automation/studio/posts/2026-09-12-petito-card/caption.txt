August 12, 2021. A witness in Moab, Utah called 911 to report a man slapping a woman outside a co-op. Police pulled the van over. On the body-cam, Gabby Petito, 22, is in tears, apologising for her own mood. Brian Laundrie is calm. Nobody was arrested.

Two weeks later a Whole Foods camera in Jackson, Wyoming caught them for the last time. On September 1 Laundrie drove the van home to Florida without her. On September 19 her remains were found at Spread Creek. The autopsy found manual strangulation.

Laundrie was found dead in a Florida reserve the next month. In his notebook he wrote that he ended her life.
