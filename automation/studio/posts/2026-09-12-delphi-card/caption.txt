Libby German recorded the man on the bridge.

February 13, 2017. Abby Williams, 13, and Libby German, 14, were dropped off at the Monon High Bridge Trail in Delphi, Indiana on a day off school. They did not come home.

Libby's phone came back with them. On it was a short video of a man walking the bridge and a voice saying three words: "Down the hill."

Five and a half years passed with no arrest. Richard Allen was arrested in October 2022, convicted in November 2024, and sentenced to 130 years. His lawyers are still arguing the jury never heard a complete defense, and on September 21, 2026 the Indiana Court of Appeals hears that argument.
