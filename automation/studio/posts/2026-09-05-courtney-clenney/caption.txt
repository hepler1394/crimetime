She said she threw the knife from across the room.

Miami, April 3, 2022. Christian Toby Obumseli, 27, died from a single stab wound in the condo he shared with Courtney Clenney, an OnlyFans model with two million followers. She called 911 and said it was self-defense. Six weeks earlier, the elevator camera in their own building had caught her hitting him. The autopsy said the wound came from a forceful downward thrust, not a throw.

She was arrested that August at a rehab facility in Hawaii and charged with second-degree murder. Then four years of delays: her parents charged over his laptop, the charges dropped, trial dates set and pushed.

On August 10, 2026, there was no trial. Clenney pleaded guilty to manslaughter with a deadly weapon. Six years, with credit for the four she has already sat in jail. Five years of probation, mandatory treatment, and she cannot make a dollar off the case. Her lawyers expect her out in under two years.
