Thirty years this December.

December 26, 1996, Boulder, Colorado. Patsy Ramsey found a ransom note on the back staircase and called police at 5:52 in the morning. Her daughter JonBenet, six years old, was found in the basement of the same house that afternoon.

The note ran to two and a half pages. Nobody has ever been charged.

In December 2025 Boulder police said they had carried out new interviews, re-interviewed people on the strength of tips, and re-tested evidence using current DNA technology. They have not said what the new evidence is. The case is open, active, and unsolved.
