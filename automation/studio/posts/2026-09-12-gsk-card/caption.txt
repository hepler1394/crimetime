"Remember when we played?"

April 6, 2001. One day after The Sacramento Bee linked the East Area Rapist to the Original Night Stalker, one of his victims answered her phone and heard those three words.

Between 1974 and 1986 the same man committed thirteen murders and more than fifty rapes across California under three different names: the Visalia Ransacker, the East Area Rapist, the Original Night Stalker. Then he stopped. For thirty-two years nobody knew who he was.

In April 2018 investigators identified his relatives through forensic genetic genealogy and worked the family tree down to a former police officer living in Citrus Heights. Joseph James DeAngelo was 72. In June 2020 he pleaded guilty to murder and kidnapping and admitted the rapes he could no longer be charged with. He is serving life without parole.
