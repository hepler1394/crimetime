The BTK Floppy Disk

October 3, 2026

Welcome back to CrimeTimeSnacks, I'm Cory. Today we're looking at one of the most infamous cold cases in American history, the BTK killer. For decades, a phantom haunted Wichita, Kansas, binding and suffocating victims while sending taunting letters to the police and media. But after going completely silent for thirteen years, the killer made a fatal mistake. He decided to reach out one more time, mailing items to law enforcement that would finally unravel his double life as a family man and security alarm installer. We'll trace the timeline from his chilling childhood fantasies to the digital footprint that brought him down.

About CrimeTimeSnacks

CrimeTimeSnacks is a true crime podcast about the cases people argue about, built from the public record: court filings, released case files and years of reporting. Each episode lays out the facts, the theories and the evidence, says what is still unknown, and leaves the conclusion to you.

Hosted by Cory. Every script is checked claim by claim against its research file before it is voiced, and every episode page carries the full transcript. How an episode gets made.
