Wilmington DMV: What We Know So Far

December 26, 2025  ·  Cory

In the hours after the December 23, 2025 incident at the Wilmington DMV, the story moved faster than the facts. This post collects what has actually been confirmed and sets aside the rest until it can be verified.

At the center of the account is Corporal Matthew Snook, a Delaware State Police officer. By the official statement, his actions in the final moments saved the lives of others present. Investigators have asked for patience as they piece together a complete timeline.
