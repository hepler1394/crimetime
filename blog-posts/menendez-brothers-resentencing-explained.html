The Menendez Resentencing, Explained

March 2, 2025  ·  Cory

Lyle and Erik Menendez were convicted in 1996 for the 1989 killings of their parents. After more than thirty-five years in prison, renewed attention has reopened questions about whether they should remain there.

The current conversation centers on resentencing and the new claims and evidence that the defense has raised. Each step is a distinct legal process with its own standard, and it is worth understanding them separately rather than as a single event.

Whatever one concludes about the case, the proceedings are a reminder of how the same set of facts can support very different stories, and how the law revisits them decades later.
