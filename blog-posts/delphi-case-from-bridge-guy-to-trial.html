Delphi: From 'Bridge Guy' to Trial

February 10, 2025  ·  Cory

On February 14, 2017, Abigail Williams and Liberty German were found near the Monon High Bridge Trail in Delphi, Indiana. For years, the most widely circulated evidence was a short clip of audio and a grainy image recorded on Libby's phone.

That fragment did something rare: it kept a difficult case in the public eye long after the early leads went cold. The phrase 'bridge guy' became shorthand for a suspect no one could yet name.

The eventual arrest and the road to trial show how modern cases can hinge on a single piece of recovered digital evidence, and on the years of patient work that follow it.
